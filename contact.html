Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you.

Foodie World is a small, independent project, and feedback from the people who use it genuinely shapes what we work on next. Whether you've spotted an error in a guide, want to suggest a cuisine or dish we should cover, or just have a question about something on the site, please get in touch.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email at hello@foodieworldrecipes.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

What to include

  • If you're reporting a correction, a link to the page and a short note about what looks wrong.
  • If you're suggesting new content, the cuisine, dish, or technique you'd like to see covered.
  • If you have a general question, as much detail as you're comfortable sharing so we can help.

Please note that Foodie World is a cooking-information website. We're not able to offer personalized nutrition, medical, or dietary advice — for those, please consult a qualified professional.
